The Industry Bottleneck

Why Current "One-Size-Fits-All" Recycling Fails

Over 70% of decommissioned solar modules still possess structurally intact low-iron tempered glass and reusable silicon cells—yet conventional plants destroy them with static batch processing.

CONVENTIONAL STATIC PIPELINE 0% Triage

Blind Thermal Heating & Mechanical Crushing

  • ✕ Destroys Intact Glass: Undamaged low-iron front glass sheets are shattered into mixed cullet worth ₹115/module instead of ₹520/module intact architectural glass.
  • ✕ Unprofitable Unit Economics: At ₹883–₹1,079/module processing cost vs. ₹610–₹890 material yield, static plants lose ₹189–₹273 on every panel.
  • ✕ Massive Energy Penalty: Heating every single module to 500°C wastes ~14.5 kWh (₹145/module at ₹10/kWh) even when zero physical damage exists.
RAYTRACE AI DYNAMIC PIPELINE 3-Tier Adaptive

Condition-Aware Laser Trajectory & Yield Routing

  • ✓ Preserves Clean Modules Whole: Full DP laser trajectory separates glass, silicon wafers, and metals intact for direct remanufacturing.
  • ✓ Surgical Isolation for Electric Faults: Perimeter-only laser cut frees the intact front glass while leaving shorted semiconductors untouched.
  • ✓ Zero Wasted Laser Cycles on Cracked Glass: Physically shattered modules immediately skip laser gantry time and go straight to cullet crushing.
